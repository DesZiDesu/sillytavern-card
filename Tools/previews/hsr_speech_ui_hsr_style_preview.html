Honkai: Star Rail Speech UI — HSR Style Study

Three thinner, right-portrait header systems based on Honkai: Star Rail’s ivory ticket surfaces, navy warp panels, gold linework, diagonal cuts, four-point stars, and clean cinematic dialogue treatment. Character portraits are loaded directly from the HSR gallery files in this repository.
